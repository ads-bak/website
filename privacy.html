Privacy Policy

BAK First LLC — Last modified: February 2025

Our company including all of its affiliates respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it through our compliance with this policy. Please read this policy carefully to understand our policies and practices regarding your information and how we will treat it.

Collection and Use of Data

Personal data is all information relating to personal and objective circumstances through which a person may be personally identified. This may include your name, address, phone number, or email address.

Intended Use

When processing your data we pursue the following legitimate interests: to present and improve our website, to estimate the size of our target group, to save information about your preferences, to speed up your search, to recognize you when you return, and to ensure the stability and security of our website.

How We Use Your Information

We use information that we collect about you or that you provide to us to present our Website, provide information and services you request, for marketing and advertising purposes, to carry out our obligations and enforce our rights, to notify you about changes, and for other purposes related to conducting business.

We may also use your information to contact you about goods and services that may be of interest to you. You may elect to unsubscribe and we will cease using your information in that manner.

Children Under the Age of 13

Our Website is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without verification of parental consent, we will delete that information.

Information We Collect

We collect several types of information from and about users who visit our Website including traffic data, location, server logs, IP address, operating system and browser type, and information collected through cookies, web beacons, and other tracking technologies.

Technologies We Use

  • Cookies: Small files placed on your device to determine whether you have visited before. You can delete cookies and object to their use through your browser settings.
  • Web Beacons: Small electronic files that permit us to count users who have visited pages or opened emails.
  • Log Files: Usage data transmitted through your browser and stored in server log files.

Information You Provide

Information provided by filling in forms on our Website, records and copies of your correspondence, details of transactions, and details of website searches and button clicks.

Tracking Technologies on Our Website

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to collect and analyze data about visitor behavior. Google Analytics uses cookies for this purpose. You can prevent data collection by downloading the browser plugin at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Google Ads

We use Google Ads to place advertisements. When you access our website via a Google ad, Google sets a cookie on your device for conversion statistics. Privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Google Remarketing

We use Google Analytics advertising features (remarketing) to display interest-based advertising. Privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. Ad settings: Google Ad Settings.

Google Signals

We use Google Signals for cross-device tracking. We only receive anonymous data. If you do not wish to be subject to this feature, you can deactivate "Ads personalization" in your Google account.

Meta Pixel

We use the "visitor action pixels" from Meta Platforms to track user behavior after clicking a Facebook ad and measure effectiveness. You can manage your ad preferences in your Facebook account settings.

LinkedIn Insight Tag

The LinkedIn Insight Tag enables campaign reporting and insights about website visitors. You can opt out of cookies from LinkedIn in your LinkedIn settings. Privacy policy: linkedin.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Microsoft Advertising

We use Universal Event Tracking (UET) via Microsoft Advertising. Privacy statement: privacy.microsoft.com. Opt out: Microsoft Ad Settings.

Disclosure of Your Information

We may disclose personal information to our subsidiaries, contractors, service providers, and other third parties we use to support our business, including web analytics providers, hosting providers, marketing providers, email providers, CRM providers, and payment processors.

We may also disclose your information to comply with legal processes, enforce our terms, or protect rights, property, or safety.

Your Choices

  • Browser settings: Set your browser to refuse cookies.
  • Consent banners: Manage cookie preferences via our consent banner.
  • Opt-out: Follow unsubscribe instructions in marketing emails.
  • NAI: Opt out of targeted ads via the Network Advertising Initiative.

How We Protect Your Data

We have implemented measures to secure your personal information from accidental loss and unauthorized access. All information is stored on secure servers behind firewalls. All transactions are encrypted using SSL technology. The transmission of information via the internet is not completely secure, and any transmission is at your own risk.

Newsletter

If you subscribe to our newsletter, we will process your email address and data to verify your subscription. You can cancel at any time by contacting us or clicking 'unsubscribe' in any email.

Data Retention

We store your data indefinitely unless otherwise specified or requested.

Changes to Our Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified on the Website home page. The date the privacy policy was last revised is identified at the top of this page.
